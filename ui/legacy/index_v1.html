Keyboard jog (WASD) and drive details

Press a key once to move, again to stop. The opposite key stops first; press it again to reverse. Each move runs to the ±80° soft limit unless stopped. Home takes both axes to 0/0 together at the current speed.

Demodulator

DVB-S2 receive chain (patched gr-dvbs2rx) with the GSSE / GSUFTP image decoder. It runs as a separate program, so a receiver crash never stops the gimbal. Receive only: it never transmits.

Track the Sun automatically

          
One button. It starts the motors, finds true north from the GNSS, works out where the Sun is,
            points the dish at it, fine-tunes on the Sun's radio noise if the SDR is plugged in, then follows it. Every step is narrated below.

  1. Nothing running. Press Track the Sun.

      
Safety: the dish focuses sunlight onto the LNB, so after 120 s on the Sun the dish moves 10° off by itself and the run ends. Press Stop (or Space / Esc) any time.
